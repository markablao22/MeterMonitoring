Privacy Policy

Mj.Ablao Meter Monitoring

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Mj.Ablao Meter Monitoring is a personal utility for recording and reviewing electricity meter readings (kWh) and water meter readings (m³).

Information and storage

The readings and entries you type into the app are stored locally on your device using the app's local browser storage. The app does not require you to create an account. The app does not send these entries to a server operated by the developer.

Sharing and disclosure

The app does not include advertising or analytics SDKs. If you choose to export a report, print it, save it as a PDF, or share a file using Android features, the information you include may be accessible to the destination or app you select. Please review that destination's privacy practices.

Retention and deletion

Your entries remain on your device until you delete them in the app or clear the app's storage/uninstall the app. Clearing storage or uninstalling may permanently remove locally saved entries. Consider exporting and safely keeping a backup of records you need.

Permissions and third-party services

The app is designed to work offline and does not require access to location, contacts, or a user account. Android system features used for printing or saving/exporting reports may be governed by Android or the selected destination app's policies.

Children's privacy

The app is a general-purpose meter-recording utility and is not designed to collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the app's features or data practices change. The latest version should be made available at this page.

Contact

For privacy questions about this app, contact the developer through the contact information provided on the app's Google Play listing.
